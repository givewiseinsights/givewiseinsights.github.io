About GiveWise Insights

Nonprofits and faith-based organizations face a real problem: they're sitting on years of donor data but don't have the tools or expertise to turn it into actionable fundraising strategy. While for-profit companies use predictive analytics to grow revenue, mission-driven organizations are left guessing which donors will give again, who's about to lapse, and where to focus limited fundraising resources.

GiveWise Insights exists to solve this gap. We built our platform specifically for organizations that need enterprise-level donor analytics without the enterprise price tag. Our models analyze giving patterns, identify high-value prospects, predict churn risk, and surface opportunities to recover lapsed donors—all using the data you already have.

The nonprofit sector loses millions each year to inefficient fundraising. Churches see declining tithes but don't know which members are at risk. Community organizations struggle to convert one-time donors into recurring supporters. We help solve these problems by giving you the same predictive insights that major fundraising operations use, delivered in reports and dashboards you can actually understand and act on.

Whether you're dealing with donor fatigue, declining retention rates, or simply want to make smarter decisions about where to invest your fundraising budget, GiveWise Insights gives you the data-driven clarity to maximize every gift and expand your mission impact.
