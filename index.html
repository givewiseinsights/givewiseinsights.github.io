About GiveWise Insights

GiveWise Insights was founded by Joseph Jilovec, an expert with a BS in Psychology and entrepreneurial background, who combines analytical frameworks with deep empathy for mission-driven organizations.

Unlike generic data tools or AI platforms, GiveWise Insights provides human expertise that understands the unique challenges churches, nonprofits, and community organizations face. Our approach recognizes that every organization has a unique mission, community, and set of challenges. We don't offer one-size-fits-all solutions. Instead, we work collaboratively to understand your specific situation and provide actionable strategies that honor your mission while growing generosity.

Whether you're dealing with declining tithes, lapsed supporters, or need help demonstrating stewardship to your board, GiveWise Insights provides the expert guidance to help you steward every gift wisely and grow your mission impact.
