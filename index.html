Before you book

A few clear answers.

Who is GiveWise Insights for?+

Owners of Phoenix-area businesses, starting with healthcare practices and professional services firms. A useful first project has a recurring administrative task, a person who owns it, and a way to check whether the change helps.

How do I book and pay for a session?+

The booking link reserves a 30-minute call at $75. Payment is arranged separately before the session. A follow-on 60-minute session at $250 is scheduled directly by agreement after the initial discussion; the 30-minute booking does not reserve an hour. Builds and upkeep need a separate written quote. The $1,500 Stripe checkout is only for the written readiness plan.

What does a $1,500–$5,000 build include?+

A separately quoted, bounded administrative workflow with agreed inputs, approvals, testing, documentation, and a handoff. The quote defines integrations, acceptance checks, revisions, delivery timing, and exclusions. Software subscriptions and usage charges are separate. Broader or sensitive-data work needs a different scope.

What does monthly upkeep cover?+

An agreed set of workflow checks, troubleshooting, and small changes. The $500–$1,500 monthly range depends on the workflows covered, service hours, response targets, and change allowance. It is optional support with written terms, not unlimited development or a promise of round-the-clock service.

Can we start without sharing patient or client records?+

Yes. Start with a high-level process description and sanitized examples. For healthcare practices, the initial focus is administrative work without patient information. Any project involving patient records or other sensitive information needs separate data-handling review, appropriate agreements, and controls before access. GiveWise does not provide clinical decisions or HIPAA certification.

How is the $1,500 readiness plan different from a build?+

The Works-With-What-You-Have AI Plan is an optional advisory engagement: a working session, review of current workflows and systems, and a written decision and testing plan. It does not include building an automation. The existing Stripe link sells that named plan only; any implementation or upkeep is agreed and quoted separately.

How does the software and telecom savings review work?+

It is a separate offer. Before any work, agree the baseline bills, service requirements, fee, measurement period, and treatment of implementation costs or service changes. Any shared-savings fee is based on the documented net savings defined in that agreement. No savings rate is promised.
