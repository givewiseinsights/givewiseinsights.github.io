Frequently asked questions.

Direct answers on data boundaries, technology ownership, and engineering practices.

Can I pay you hourly so we can have 14 alignment meetings first? +
Respectfully, no. Hourly billing rewards inefficiency, endless slide decks, and talking in circles. We quote fixed-scope milestone deliverables so you know exactly what is being built, when it goes live, and what it costs down to the dollar before any work starts. If you love calendar invitations more than shipping working software, we are probably not the right team for you.
Can't I just hire an unpaid intern or my nephew who “knows ChatGPT”? +
You certainly can! Just ensure your legal department is comfortable with confidential customer records floating around in an unencrypted consumer browser session, and your operations manager is prepared to spend 25 hours a week fixing hallucinated spreadsheets. When that novelty wears off, give us a call to build real, deterministic software.
Will these autonomous AI agents replace my team and turn into Skynet? +
Not even close. Our systems are built to eliminate soul-crushing repetitive busywork—like manual data re-entry, triage, and document hunting—so your humans can focus on revenue and client relationships. Think of it as giving your team high-speed digital assistants with infinite caffeine, not replacing your staff.
How do your automations integrate with our existing legacy software and CRM? +
We integrate directly via official REST APIs, secure Webhooks, database connectors (Postgres, SQL Server, Supabase), and custom middleware. If your legacy software lacks an open API, we engineer deterministic background worker services to safely bridge data without forcing you into an expensive rip-and-replace overhaul.
How long does a typical implementation take from start to production? +
The initial Readiness Audit & Architecture Blueprint takes 1–2 weeks. Once technical specifications and guardrails are approved, targeted workflow builds generally take 3–5 weeks to deploy into staging, verify with human-in-the-loop testing, and roll out to your live operations.
What level of technical expertise does our internal team need to run this? +
Zero coding skills required. We build clean, intuitive interfaces and automated dashboards so your non-technical staff can operate, review, and monitor tasks seamlessly. And with our Retained Upkeep, our engineers handle all API maintenance, security patches, and performance tuning behind the scenes.
Do you train models on our proprietary business data? +
Never. We exclusively deploy zero-data-retention enterprise API endpoints and client-isolated vector environments. Your proprietary data, client lists, and internal records are never used to train public models. All code, prompts, and database configurations remain 100% your property.
How do you prevent AI hallucinations in business operations? +
We design deterministic guardrails using Pydantic schema validation, multi-agent cross-verification, and strict confidence threshold scoring. If an extracted record or decision falls below 98% confidence, the pipeline halts and routes the item to an authorized staff member with the exact source citation highlighted.
What is included in the $3,500 Readiness Audit & Architecture Blueprint? +
You receive a deep 1–2 week inspection of candidate workflows: technical API feasibility, data cleanliness audit, security/compliance posture, human-in-the-loop review criteria, and a comprehensive fixed-price build specification. If you proceed with the build through GiveWise, 100% of the audit fee is credited toward the build.
What happens if an API or third-party tool changes after launch? +
This is precisely why we offer our Monthly Retained Upkeep ($2,500–$4,500/mo). We actively monitor uptime, patch breaking API changes from vendors like OpenAI, Anthropic, or your CRM, fine-tune prompts to prevent performance degradation, and maintain a monthly queue for new micro-automations.
Who operates GiveWise Insights? +
GiveWise Insights is a specialized enterprise AI transformation and automation advisory practice operated as a commercial trade name of Jentlemens LLC, based in Mesa, Arizona.
