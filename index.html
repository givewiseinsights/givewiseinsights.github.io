Before you book

A few clear answers.

How is the free review different from the $1,500 plan?+

The free review is a 30-minute conversation and a one-page opportunity list. The paid plan goes deeper into your workflows and systems, compares costs and benefits, identifies preparation work and approval rules, and gives you a written testing and handoff plan.

Does the plan include building an automation?+

No. The $1,500 plan is a one-time advisory engagement. Development, integration, software subscriptions, and ongoing operations are separate. You can take the plan to your own team or an implementation partner.

Do we need to buy new software?+

We start with the tools and data you already have. If an additional tool is worth considering, the plan identifies its cost and what it would need to do. Choosing not to add AI is a valid outcome.

What should we bring to the first call?+

One recurring task, the tools you use, who owns it, and a rough sense of the time it takes. A high-level description is enough for the free review. Any later access or document sharing is agreed separately; don't put credentials or confidential customer information in the booking form.

What happens after I buy the plan?+

You'll receive an email within one business day to arrange the working session. The scope, exclusions, information needed, and delivery schedule are confirmed in writing before work begins. Start with the free review if you'd like to check fit first.
