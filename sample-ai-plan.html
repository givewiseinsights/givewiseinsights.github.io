Owner-Run Commercial Practice (45 FTEs) / Existing ERP, Shared Document Stores & CRM

Executive Strategic Directive

Deploy deterministic agentic pipeline for routine client portfolio documentation and reconciliation. Human-in-the-loop validation remains strictly mandated prior to external dispatch.

  1. Deploy

    Automate Client Reporting & Reconciliation

    Ingest verified account data from ERP, synthesize status briefs through structured prompts, and stage in approval queues. Zero unsupervised client transmissions.

  2. Engineer

    Deterministic Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)

    Establish strict role-based access controls, document hygiene protocols, and vector indexing across contract repositories before enabling semantic search.

  3. Defer

    Unrestricted Model Autonomy on Financials

    Prohibit direct autonomous execution on credit terms, contractual discounting, or wire authorizations. Preserve executive discretion and cryptographic logs.
